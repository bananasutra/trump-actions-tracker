# Dataset licensing and attribution

This file covers the `trump-actions*.csv` files only. For the dashboard
software in this repository, see [`LICENSE`](LICENSE).

## Source and credit

The dataset is the **Trump Action Tracker**, collected, described and
classified by **Professor Christina Pagel** and published at
[trumpactiontracker.info](https://www.trumpactiontracker.info/).

It is not authored by this repository. It is included here as a dated
archive, credited to her, under her stated permission for journalism,
research, education and other public-interest analysis.

## Terms, verbatim

Each export carries its licensing statement on line 1 of the CSV. Those
statements are reproduced here exactly as published.

> republish or redistribute the dataset in whole or in substantial part
> without prior written permission. Source: trumpactiontracker.info.

Earlier exports (`trump-actions.csv`, `trump-actions-3-1-26.csv`,
`trump-actions-3-4-26.csv`, `trump-actions-3-24-26.csv`):

> Trump Action Tracker — ©️ The authors Christina Pagel, Creative Commons
> CC BY 4.0 license (data) (site content). Source:
> trumpactiontracker.info.

## Why the terms differ between files

Upstream changed the licence between exports. Files up to and including
`trump-actions-3-24-26.csv` state **CC BY 4.0**. From
`trump-actions-5-16-26.csv` onward they state **CC BY-SA 4.0** and add
the redistribution clause above.

The historical files are deliberately left as published. Each export is
an archival snapshot, and rewriting the licence text inside one would
misrepresent what upstream actually stated on that date. Always read
line 1 of the specific file you are using.

## If you want to reuse this data

Go to [trumpactiontracker.info](https://www.trumpactiontracker.info/) and
use the export there, crediting Professor Pagel. Note that her terms
distinguish *use* for public-interest analysis, which is granted with
credit, from *republication or redistribution of the dataset in whole or
in substantial part*, which requires prior written permission from her.
